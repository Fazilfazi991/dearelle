Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order from outside India?

Yes. You can order from GCC or anywhere else and add your loved one's India delivery address.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we deliver across India based on serviceable pincodes.

Can I add a personal note?

Yes. You can add a personal message while ordering or through WhatsApp.

Can I upload a photo?

Yes, selected boxes support photo card upload.

Can I hide the price?

Yes. For gift boxes, price details should be hidden by default.

Can I choose a delivery date?

Yes, customers can request a preferred delivery date. Final delivery depends on location and courier timelines.

What comes inside each box?

Each box includes selected jewellery and gift items. Some boxes include chocolates, cosmetics, photo cards, and premium packaging.

Can I customize the box?

Yes, customization is available through selected add-ons or WhatsApp support.

How does monthly surprise work?

In Phase 1, monthly surprise is handled through WhatsApp/manual confirmation. Automatic subscription billing can be added later.

Can I order through WhatsApp?

Yes. Customers can order or customize their gift box through WhatsApp.
